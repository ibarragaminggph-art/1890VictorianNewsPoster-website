Ang Pang-araw-araw na Pabalitaan

Inililimbag Araw-araw sa Kapakanan ng Katotohanan, Kalakalan, at mga Bagong Katha.

PARA SA MAMAMAYAN NG CIUDAD DE PASEO!

Y esterday afternoon, a momentous trial of the new electric street railway took place amidst throngs of cheering spectators. Engineers report flawless conduction along the primary copper lines. Critics who once doubted the feasibility of harnessing invisible galvanic currents are now forced to reconsider the dawn of a new mechanical age.

Municipal authorities have confirmed that expansion to the uptown districts shall commence before the first winter frost. Shareholders are advised to secure their certificates promptly at the downtown exchange.

MYSTERIOUS AIRSHIP SIGHTED OVER THE HARBOR

Dozens of startled dockworkers reported witnessing a cigar-shaped vessel gliding silently through the night clouds propelled by roaring propellers of unknown design.
